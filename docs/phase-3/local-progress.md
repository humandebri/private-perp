## Installed

- Set the shared REST budget for policy from guard, and register vault/core as workers in bootstrap. Both workers' HL REST transmission paths are obtained before sending policy permission. They do not start sending when the permission expires or policy fails.
- Orders, allocation, and Agent approval are counted as new risks, cancellation, withdrawal, and recovery as exits, and reconciliation is counted as reconciliation. HL's `userFillsByTime` ensures a weight of 120 for a maximum of 2000 transactions. Positions and inquiries about order status are ensured with a weight of 2. 1200 per minute and 300 exit reservations are local test values and will be adjusted after actual measurement.
- core's sweep is processed in the order of cancellation, reconciliation, and orders. An account with unresolved orders is prioritized in a different permanent cursor, while the regular rolling cursor is also maintained. The interval for `userFillsByTime` retrieval is 2 minutes for accounts with unresolved orders, and 10 minutes for others, and the final retrieval time is retained even after upgrading.
- Reduce-only orders are excluded from emergency suspension and the new symbol allowlist, and unnecessary `updateLeverage` transmissions are avoided. Do not resend processing of POSTunknown outcome, but leave it in the existing unknownreconciliation path.
- The vault's outbox transitions to the send state after obtaining budget permission. To prevent action from being left in the signature-completed state due to permission acquisition failure, it can be retried after the lease expires. When there is a rejection or uncertainty, code the audit reasons, deposit reconciliation failure, and order retry reasons, and do not save the trading exchange's live messages or account addresses in audit events.
- We added verification to the guard permissions and retry/single-dispatch email after budget rejection in PocketIC. We updated the Candid and frontend vault binding.
- Recovery requests, trading balance reservation, nonce, and action are combined into a single DB transaction. The core account fence is only operated by vault, and prepare checks local orders and HL's openOrders and positions, and after commit confirms the state of transmission and POST. Cancellation and reduce-only settlement can also be used while the fence is in use.
- If HL explicitly indicates acceptance or rejection of `usdSend`, use that response to confirm the recovery accounting, reservation, and action, and retry the core's fence removal from the permanent queue. If you miss a POST response, do not resend; retain the reservation and fence.
- The transfer history is retrieved by page using `startTime` and `endTime`, and the transfer source, destination, amount, time, and hash are reconciled. When the limit of 500 items is reached, the window is reduced and re-retrieved; if the query fails or the match is ambiguous, it is retained as unknown. The non-execution judgment is limited to only when the testnet history integrity verification is explicitly set after approximately 2 days of nonce verification and full page confirmation. If the network or HL endpoint is changed, the verification settings will automatically be invalidated.
- Convert the `usdc` in HL history from both JSON numbers and strings to microUSDC using integer arithmetic. It is unified through deposit traversal and controllerreconciliation, and unknownreconciliation for recovery, and does not treat amounts that require rounding, those outside the range, or invalid types as grounds for rejection. The conversion function retains the original JSON type.
- The core upgrade completely stops new risks, and the vault will be moved to a generation-based fence for unresolved recovery before being lifted. If the fence lift fails, it will be retried from the permanent action. The user's `FundStatus` and the screen will display "Recovery preparation in progress" or "Reconciliation waiting".
- Add an independent `send_journal` canister and record the request ID, account ID, nonce, and digest before the HL POST in the vault/core to a hash chain. The sender stores the receipt sequence and hash in the same DB transaction as `dispatching`, and reconciles the high water mark of the independent journal before each transmission. If there is no receipt record for an old action that is not received due to journal failure, sequence mismatch, or unresolved issues, stop the new POST. The scope of record retrieval is limited to the registered worker themselves, with a maximum of 100 records per time. It has been added to the local bootstrap and Candid.
- Deposits from HL history are first recorded in advance in the V2 business event without revealing the tx hash, network, recipient, amount, and observation time, and then the receipt number and ledger accounting are finalized in the same DB transaction. Duplicate tx hashes are not re-counted or re-recorded. History types other than deposits are not counted only by the actual amount, and recovery transfers under management are also excluded. Direct deposit injections by controllers are reserved for `test-venue` and have been removed from the live Candid.
- For direct debit to an unknown deposit address, the target event ID, personal ID, and amount are pre-added to the V2 business event in a non-public manner, and the transaction from the receipt number and suspension to the personal balance is finalized in the same DB transaction. We verify the uncharged status before and after the addition, and reject balance changes due to duplicate charges or journal discrepancies.

Within the entry fence, reconfirm the existing personal ID and finalize the receipt number and personal bank account in the same database transaction. ## Migrating existing environments

Upgrade `send_journal` before vault so it understands `RecoveryPostResult`. Old `RecoverySettlement` cannot distinguish direct responses from history decisions; if it appears in restoration deltas, retain the send lock. Upgrading core first stops all new risk. Then upgrade vault, configure the core principal initially, and run the vault sweep. Vault moves unresolved legacy recoveries to generation-bearing fences and unlocks core's migration only when all have moved. Inspect `recovery_migration_locked` and the user's `FundStatus.recovery_fence`. Cancellation and reduce-only closes remain available during migration. Do not enable `recovery_history_verified` on testnet before verifying HL history completeness.
